About these captions

  • The captions are made by speech recognition (AI) running on the lecturer's computer. The audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • They can contain mistakes, especially technical terms, names and numbers.
  • This page does not ask who you are and the server stores nothing about you. Your text size and colours are saved only in this browser.
  • When the lecturer clears the screen, the text disappears here too. When the session ends, the server deletes the captions.
